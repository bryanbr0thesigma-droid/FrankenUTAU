Corrupt-Chan 3D (fan build)
- corrupt_chan_body.vrm: body/outfit (VRoid sample "Darkness Shibu", CC0), repainted.
- corrupt_chan_hair.vrm: hair (VRoid sample "Sendagaya Shino", CC0), hair textures neutralised.
- cc_build.js: three.js + three-vrm 2.x code that combines the two, moves the hair onto the body,
  applies the hair gradient, adds the Adachi Rei-style headset, knee seams and glitch flashes.
Credits: Corrupt-Chan by mono23; headset after Adachi Rei by missile39; design by bryanbr0.
